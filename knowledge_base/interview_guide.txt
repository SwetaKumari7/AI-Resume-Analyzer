INTERVIEW PREPARATION GUIDE

Resume Questions:

1. Tell me about yourself.
2. Explain your most important project.
3. What problem does your project solve?
4. What was your contribution?
5. What challenges did you face?
6. How did you test your solution?
7. What would you improve?

Technical Questions:

Programming:
Data structures, algorithms, OOP, exception handling,
debugging and complexity analysis.

Database:
SQL queries, joins, normalization, indexes and transactions.

Web:
HTTP, REST APIs, authentication and client-server architecture.

AI/ML:
Training and testing, overfitting, evaluation metrics,
embeddings, LLMs and retrieval augmented generation.

Behavioral:
Teamwork, learning from mistakes, handling deadlines,
communication and problem solving.

Interview Preparation Principle:

Candidates should explain the problem first,
then the approach, then the technologies used,
and finally the result.
