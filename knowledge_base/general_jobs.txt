GENERAL JOBS CAREER GUIDE

This knowledge base covers common technical and non-technical
employment roles. Recommendations must be based on the user's
actual resume and the requirements of the target job.

HOUSEKEEPER / DOMESTIC WORKER

Common Responsibilities:
Cleaning rooms and household areas, maintaining cleanliness,
washing dishes, laundry, organizing household items,
following household instructions and assisting with daily tasks.

Important Skills:
Cleaning and organization, time management, reliability,
attention to detail, following instructions, responsibility,
basic communication and maintaining workplace hygiene.

Common Qualities:
Trustworthiness, punctuality, patience, hardworking attitude,
respect for privacy and ability to work independently.

Typical Resume Evidence:
Housekeeping experience, domestic work, hotel cleaning,
hospitality work, cleaning responsibilities or similar experience.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited experience with professional cleaning procedures,
time management, workplace safety, hygiene practices or
communication with employers.

RETAIL WORKER

Common Responsibilities:
Customer assistance, product arrangement, billing support,
inventory handling, store cleanliness and basic sales support.

Important Skills:
Customer service, communication, basic mathematics,
organization, teamwork, product knowledge and reliability.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited customer service experience, cash handling,
inventory management or communication experience.

RECEPTIONIST / FRONT DESK

Common Responsibilities:
Greeting visitors, answering calls, scheduling appointments,
maintaining records and assisting customers.

Important Skills:
Communication, customer service, organization,
basic computer skills, telephone etiquette and time management.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited office software experience, communication practice,
appointment management or front-desk experience.

OFFICE ASSISTANT

Common Responsibilities:
Data entry, document organization, answering calls,
maintaining records and assisting office staff.

Important Skills:
Basic computer skills, typing, MS Office or similar tools,
organization, communication, accuracy and time management.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited spreadsheet experience, document management,
data entry or professional communication.

CUSTOMER SERVICE

Common Responsibilities:
Helping customers, answering questions,
resolving basic problems, handling complaints and
maintaining customer records.

Important Skills:
Communication, patience, problem solving,
active listening, empathy, basic computer skills
and professionalism.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited customer interaction, complaint handling,
CRM experience or professional communication.

SALES ASSOCIATE

Common Responsibilities:
Customer interaction, explaining products,
supporting sales, maintaining product displays
and achieving sales targets.

Important Skills:
Communication, customer service, persuasion,
product knowledge, confidence and teamwork.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited sales experience, product presentation,
customer handling or target-based work.

RESTAURANT / HOSPITALITY WORKER

Common Responsibilities:
Customer service, table assistance, maintaining
cleanliness, food service support and teamwork.

Important Skills:
Communication, cleanliness, punctuality,
customer service, teamwork and ability to work
in a fast-paced environment.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited hospitality experience, customer handling,
workplace hygiene or teamwork experience.

DRIVER

Common Responsibilities:
Safe transportation, vehicle care,
following routes, maintaining punctuality
and basic documentation.

Important Skills:
Valid driving qualification where required,
road safety awareness, punctuality,
responsibility, navigation and basic vehicle knowledge.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited professional driving experience,
route planning, vehicle maintenance knowledge
or professional customer interaction.

SECURITY / SECURITY GUARD

Common Responsibilities:
Monitoring premises, checking visitors,
following security procedures, reporting incidents
and maintaining awareness.

Important Skills:
Alertness, responsibility, communication,
discipline, observation and ability to follow procedures.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited security experience, incident reporting,
access control or workplace safety knowledge.

GENERAL EMPLOYMENT PRINCIPLE

For non-technical jobs, a resume does not need to contain
large numbers of technical skills.

Relevant experience, reliability, communication,
responsibility, punctuality, practical skills and
job-specific experience can be important evidence.

The assistant must never invent experience.

If a skill is not present in the resume, it should be
described as a possible gap rather than as something
the user definitely lacks.

For users with limited formal work experience,
the assistant should identify transferable skills
and suggest realistic ways to demonstrate them.

CAR WASHER / VEHICLE CLEANER

Common Responsibilities:
Washing and cleaning vehicles, cleaning vehicle interiors
and exteriors, vacuuming, drying vehicles, maintaining
cleaning equipment and keeping the work area clean.

Important Skills:
Vehicle cleaning, attention to detail, physical stamina,
time management, careful handling of vehicles,
equipment handling and workplace cleanliness.

Common Qualities:
Punctuality, reliability, responsibility, hardworking attitude,
carefulness and ability to follow instructions.

Possible Skill Gaps:
Limited professional vehicle cleaning experience,
limited knowledge of cleaning equipment, workplace safety,
vehicle care procedures or customer interaction.

Transferable Skills:
Cleaning experience, physical work, attention to detail,
punctuality, responsibility and maintaining an organized
work area can be relevant evidence.

Resume Principle:
Do not assume the user has car washing experience.
If it is not present in the resume, describe it as a
possible skill or experience gap rather than inventing it.
